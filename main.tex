\begin{abstract}
These are lecture notes for a first course in Commutative Algebra, covering rings, ideals, and modules, together with the accompanying problem-set discussions worked out alongside the lectures. The standard references for this material are \cite{AtiyahMacdonald}, \cite{Blyth1990}, and \cite{DummitFoote2004}.
\end{abstract}
